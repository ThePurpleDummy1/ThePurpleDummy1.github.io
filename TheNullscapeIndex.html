Welcome to the index!

A list of the enemies/upgrades/classes, what they do, and how to survive the absurd amount of enemies! (In that order) Also i wont be going over curses because then this page would be 10 pages long.

So Lets Get Started From The Easiest Enemy's!

Starting with everyone's favorite blue blob...

MART!

Mart is to be described as a blue blob that floats in the void that your trapped in. upon hitting him he will end your run because even though he looks cute he doesnt show mercy! Look below to learn how to avoid.

  • look around, he glows a faint blue color
  • listen for what i can describe as a snakes rattle
  • If he appears infrount of you jump over or around him
  • if there are multiple marts they can combine and become both faster and bigger, increacing the size of his hitbox

This is not all of the information about mart because i wont be going over curses because there is just SO MANY.

Anyways moving on to the next enemy...

Operator!!!

this little guy is what i like to call a small learning curve! when he shows up you must stand still when his face turns from green to yellow (almost instant) so watch out for that :3

  • After a short amount of time operator will show up during the round, so be ready
  • upon spawning they will turn yellow, after a certain amount of time (depending on the dificulty) you will die unless you stop moving or using an ability in the short time frame
  • you will know they left when their face turns green, then they will disappear untill they show up again.

i cant explain operator any more until later on because i plan on going over a certain enemy type later.

Anyways who's ready for everyone's least favorite trackstar...

Husk

Husk's concept is simple, when he spawns in he mimics your EXACT inputs with a one second delay (curses can make this delay up to 1/2 the time or double) and hitting him, just like all the other enemys in this game, will kill you instantly!

  • After a few seconds of making your first imput into a round husk will spawn with a respective little jingle
  • Remember, husk mimics your every movement with a delay, so just don't retrace your steps and go somewhere else for a second.

Just remember to not take the same path and you should be fine!
